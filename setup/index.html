Profile Sharing Setup

This guide explains how teams can share browser profiles without creating avoidable exposure. The safest approach is to keep each profile isolated, control access by role, and avoid unnecessary switching between accounts.

That structure helps prevent account bans while supporting smoother agency account management across departments and client teams.

Best practice: keep one profile per workflow and one owner per sensitive account.
